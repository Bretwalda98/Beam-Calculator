Beam Calculator Studio

Version 34.2.1 • Local single-file engineering workspace.

Calculation basis

Load combinations follow EN 1990. Steel resistance and stability checks follow the implemented EN 1993 methods shown in Help and the generated report.

Section data

Published section properties are sourced from ArcelorMittal, British Steel and Steel for Life Blue Book data where available.

Privacy and licence

Preferences are stored locally in this browser. No project inputs are uploaded by the calculator. See the included LICENSE file for project licensing.
