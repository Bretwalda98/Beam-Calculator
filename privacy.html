Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 June 2026

Beam Calculator Studio is an engineering web application. This policy explains what information is used by the application and by advertising services on this site.

Local project data

Project inputs, saved drafts, theme settings and interface preferences may be stored locally in your browser using local storage. Local storage remains on your device unless you clear it or use the app's project delete/reset controls.

Calculation service

When you run a calculation, generate a report, or generate a hand calculation, the app sends the current beam inputs to the Beam Calculator Studio backend so the server can perform the calculation and return results. Do not enter confidential project information unless you are comfortable sending it to the calculation service.

Advertising and cookies

This site may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Users can also learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services at Google's partner sites policy page.

Other third party vendors or ad networks may also use cookies to serve ads on this site. You may visit those vendors' websites to opt out of personalised advertising where they offer that option, or visit aboutads.info for industry opt-out information.

Analytics and diagnostics

The application may use standard browser and server logs for security, reliability and troubleshooting. These logs can include technical information such as request time, URL, browser type, IP-derived region and error information.

Engineering disclaimer

Beam Calculator Studio outputs must be checked by a competent engineer before design, fabrication, construction or approval use. The application is not a substitute for professional engineering judgement.
